I am an Assistant Professor in the Deprtment of Environmental and Occupational Health Sciences at the University of Washington. Broadly I'm interested in how environmental conditions affect human health and well-being. To date my specific research has focused on understanding how climate and land use impact vector-borne diseases and disentangling the impact of wildfire smoke on air pollution in the US. I previously was postdoctoral fellow at the Harvard University Center for the Environment and in the Departments of Nutritions and Environmental Health at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. I recieved my PhD from Stanford University in the [Emmett Interdisciplinary Program in Environment and Resources](https://earth.stanford.edu/eiper) and was coadvised by [Erin Mordecai](https://www.mordecailab.com/) in the Department of Biology and [Marshall Burke](https://www.stanfordecholab.com/) in the Department of Earth System Science.
